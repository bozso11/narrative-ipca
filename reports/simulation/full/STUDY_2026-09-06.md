# Simulation study summary (2026-09-06)

Tuning variants: `bks` = BKS in-sample MVE Sharpe, exact argmax; `tol02` = same criterion, sparsest point within 2% of the maximum; `loocv` = leave-one-period-out Sharpe (BKS App. C.3) on 16 subsampled folds. Values are mean ± std over seeds. Metric definitions: DESIGN.md Part E.

Check set v2 (DESIGN.md D52): `gamma_subspace_cos`, `state_canonical_corr`, `impact_spearman` are reported only (not identified targets of the model: Gamma_tilde is identified only up to an (L - K)-dimensional family); the recall checks are soft (a sparse representative may use only the strong topics); under `no_factor` the placebo count is reported against its chance level n_placebo / L, not checked.
